I Just Purchased Microsoft 365—Now What? Install, Reinstall, Activate & Sign In Step by Step

Just purchased Microsoft 365 and wondering what happens next? Follow this practical step-by-step guide to connect your purchase to your account, install the applications, activate your subscription, sign in, and handle a reinstall when necessary.

Microsoft 365 Install Activation Sign In Reinstall

I Just Purchased Microsoft 365. What Should I Do First?

If you are thinking, “I just purchased Microsoft 365, how do I install it?”, you are not alone. The first step is not usually to start downloading random installation files. Instead, identify the Microsoft account connected with your purchase and make sure you know which version or subscription you bought.

Microsoft 365 setup generally follows a straightforward path: purchase, account sign-in, installation, activation, and then use of the applications. Keeping these steps in order helps avoid common problems such as installing the wrong product or signing in with a different account.

Start with the account: If your purchase is already associated with a Microsoft account, use that same account during setup and activation. Switching accounts can make a valid purchase appear to be missing.

Step 1: Confirm Your Purchase and Microsoft Account

Before installing anything, check the purchase confirmation and identify the Microsoft account used during checkout or redemption. This is especially important if you have multiple email addresses or Microsoft accounts.

You may see people refer to this step as Microsoft 365 login my account, Microsoft 365 sign in account, or simply Microsoft account sign in. These searches generally point to the same basic task: access the account associated with your Microsoft 365 purchase.

  • Check the email address used for the purchase.
  • Make sure you can sign in to that Microsoft account.
  • Keep your purchase confirmation available.
  • If you received a product key, keep it nearby.

Step 2: Redeem a Product Key if Your Purchase Includes One

Not every Microsoft 365 purchase requires you to manually enter a product key during installation. Some purchases are already linked to an account. Others may provide a key that needs to be redeemed before the subscription or product becomes available.

If you have a key, use the appropriate Microsoft redemption process and sign in with the account you want associated with the purchase. This is sometimes described as Microsoft 365 product key activation or activate Microsoft 365 with product key.

For a detailed walkthrough, see our Microsoft 365 setup product key guide .

Step 3: Install Microsoft 365 on Your Computer

Once your account and purchase are ready, locate the installation option associated with your subscription. Download the installer and run it on your computer.

  1. Sign in to the account connected to your purchase. Verify that the subscription or product you purchased is visible.
  2. Select the install option. Choose the available installation command for your Microsoft 365 subscription.
  3. Download the installer. Allow the installation package to download completely before opening it.
  4. Run the installer. Follow the on-screen instructions and allow the installation to complete.
  5. Open an application. Launch Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or another included application to continue setup.

If you want instructions specifically for both operating systems, use our Microsoft 365 PC or Mac installation guide .

Step 4: Sign In After Installation

Installation places the applications on your computer, but you may still need to sign in before the subscription is fully activated. When an application asks you to sign in, use the same Microsoft account connected to your purchase.

If you accidentally sign in with another account, the application may show an activation problem or indicate that no eligible subscription is available. Sign out and verify the account before assuming that the purchase itself is defective.

Step 5: Activate Your Microsoft 365 Subscription

Activation confirms that your installed applications can use the subscription associated with your account. If you are asking, “How do I activate my Microsoft Office subscription?”, the answer depends on how the product was purchased and linked.

For many Microsoft 365 purchases, activation is completed by signing in to the correct Microsoft account after installation. If your purchase came with a product key, redemption may be required first.

Avoid entering a product key repeatedly if it has already been redeemed. Once a key is connected to an account, the account itself can become the important part of accessing the purchase later.

What If You Need to Reinstall Microsoft 365?

Reinstallation can become necessary after replacing a computer, repairing a system, removing a damaged installation, or troubleshooting application problems.

If you need to Reinstall Microsoft 365 already purchased, start with the Microsoft account associated with your original purchase. You generally do not want to purchase another subscription simply because the application is no longer installed on your computer.

Our dedicated Reinstall Microsoft 365 already purchased guide covers the process in more detail.

What If Microsoft 365 Was Already Installed?

If the applications are already on your computer, you may not need to perform a completely new installation. Open an application and check which account is currently signed in. A sign-in or activation issue can sometimes be related to the account rather than the installation itself.

Also check whether an older or different edition is installed. Multiple installations or leftover versions can sometimes make troubleshooting more complicated.

Common Problems After Purchasing Microsoft 365

The subscription does not appear

Confirm that you are signed in to the same Microsoft account used when purchasing or redeeming the subscription. If you have several accounts, try to identify the exact purchase account rather than creating another one.

The product key does not work

Carefully check the characters and make sure the key belongs to the product you purchased. If the key was already redeemed, look for the purchase under the associated account instead of attempting to redeem it again.

The application says activation is required

Open the account section inside the application and verify the signed-in account. A valid subscription connected to another account will not necessarily activate an installation signed in under the wrong account.

You purchased a different product

Microsoft 365 subscriptions and one-time Office purchases are not the same type of product. If you purchased a one-time edition such as Home & Student, follow the setup process for that product rather than assuming that every installation screen is identical.

For a Home & Student installation, see our Home & Student 2021/2024 setup guide .

Quick Microsoft 365 Setup Checklist

  • Confirm what Microsoft 365 product or subscription you purchased.
  • Identify the Microsoft account connected to the purchase.
  • Locate your product key if one was supplied.
  • Redeem the key if the purchase requires redemption.
  • Sign in to your account before downloading.
  • Download and install Microsoft 365.
  • Open an included application.
  • Sign in with the correct account.
  • Complete activation.
  • Keep your account details available if you need to reinstall later.
